# Privacy Policy — Grub

**Last Updated:** March 15, 2026

## Overview

Grub is a virtual pet game that runs entirely on your device. We respect your privacy and have designed the app to function without collecting, storing, or transmitting any personal data.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any data.** Specifically:

- No personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- No usage analytics or tracking
- No device identifiers
- No location data
- No cookies or similar technologies
- No advertising data

## Data Storage

All game data (your pet's name, stats, inventory, and progress) is stored **locally on your device only**. This data is never transmitted to any server or third party.

If you delete the app, all game data is permanently removed.

## Third-Party Services

Grub does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or social media APIs.

## Children's Privacy

Grub does not collect any data from any user, including children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
